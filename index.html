How buying works

You decide before we spend a naira on your car. Most cars reach you about 6 to 8 weeks after your first payment.

  1. Send a request

    Pick one of our cars, describe the one you want, or send us a link to a car you found. Requests are free.

  2. Our US team checks it

    We inspect the car, verify the title and history, and vet the seller. Then we send the full price, payment plan and delivery cost.

  3. Pay in full or in instalments

    Your first payment lets us buy the car. On instalment plans, the rest is due at set stages.

  4. Track every stage

    We handle the purchase, towing to the US port, shipping and customs. Your tracking code shows where your car is the whole way.

  5. Receive your car

    Pick it up in Lagos or have it delivered to your location. Any balance is paid before handover.

Questions buyers ask

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply the same day.

Are the cars accident-free?

Yes. We only bring in cars with a clean US title and no accident record. You get the history report to check for yourself before you pay.

Who checks the car before you buy it?

Our own team in the US. They inspect the vehicle, check its history report, verify the title and vet the seller before any money changes hands. If a car doesn't pass, we don't buy it. See how our validation works.

I found a car online. Can you bring it in for me?

Yes. Send us the link. We confirm the seller is genuine and that we can import the car, then send you a quote covering the purchase, shipping, customs and delivery. Once you pay, we start, and you can track the car the same way as our own listings.

How do instalment plans work?

Each car shows the plans we accept, such as 60% / 40%. The first part is paid before we buy the car. The rest is due at set stages, for example when the car arrives in Nigeria. The full amount is settled before handover.

Can you deliver to my city?

Yes. You can pick up your car in Lagos, or we can deliver it to your location. We include the delivery cost in your quote.

Do I pay anything to send a request?

No. A request just tells us you're interested. We confirm everything with you before asking for any payment.

How long until I get my car?

Usually about 6 to 8 weeks from your first payment. US paperwork or port delays can add time, and you'll see any delay on your tracking page.

How do I follow my car's progress?

Once your first payment is confirmed, we send you a tracking code. Enter it on the Track your car page to see each stage, your payments and our latest updates.

What if the final cost changes?

Exchange rates and customs charges can move while a car is on the way. We'll explain exactly how price changes are handled when we confirm your car, before you pay.
