We bring in cars we'd drive ourselves

Buying a car in Nigeria shouldn't feel like a gamble. We started MooEmma Motors to give buyers a better option: a clean tokunbo car with a known history, an honest price, flexible payment, and a clear view of every step.

Why we started

Too many people pay good money for a Nigerian-used car, only to find out weeks later what the last owner never mentioned. Our roads are hard on cars, and proper servicing is often skipped. By the time a car changes hands here, its real history is usually lost.

The US has a huge market of well-kept used cars with clean titles and full service records. Bought carefully, they make excellent cars at fair prices, even after shipping and duty.

That's what we do. We pick the cars carefully, show you their history before you commit, and keep you informed until the keys are in your hand. If you've found a car yourself, we'll check it and bring it in for you the same way.

What we promise every buyer

The truth about every car

Our team in the US checks every car before we buy it, and you see the photos and history report before you pay anything. If we wouldn't buy it, we won't sell it to you.

The price before we buy

We confirm the full price, including delivery to your location, before asking you to pay. No surprise charges at the end.

You're carried along

You get a tracking code the day your first payment is confirmed. Check it any time to see where your car is, with dates, photos and notes.

Payment that fits

Instalment plans on selected cars, with a clear schedule showing what's due and when. You see every payment on your tracking page.

Cars we won't buy

A low price isn't the same as a good buy. Whether it's one of our cars or one you found, we walk away from:

  • Accident or salvage cars. We only bring in cars with a clean title and no accident record.
  • Flood-damaged cars. Water causes electrical faults that show up months later.
  • Histories that don't add up. Odometer gaps or unexplained records are a reason to pass.
  • Sellers we can't verify. If we can't confirm a seller is genuine, we won't send your money.
